# Spires

Each Spire has one damage keyword and 20 floors with fixed enemy levels and permanent completion.

Both selected party members must have a matching keyword among any of their Basic,
Skill, or Ultimate ability choices, including conditional damage. Attunement does
not depend on the equipped cards or talent affinities. Full Game access and floor
progression remain separate requirements; free players can enter the first ten
floors under the [access contract](Monetization.md#access).

Every floor receives one node modifier from its Spire keyword's damage bonus, resistance, or guaranteed keyword equipment reward. These three choices have equal odds. The player's world seed, Spire ID, and floor number fix the result across previews, retries, and relaunches; no modifier data is added to the save. Only the current floor shows its modifier on the climb screen. Inspecting its enemy shows the same modifier used for enemy effects, battle details, and loot. A keyword equipment reward keeps the Spire's usual keyword loot bias and guarantees the matching keyword on the awarded item.
