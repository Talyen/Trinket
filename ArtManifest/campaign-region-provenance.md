# Campaign region artwork provenance

The project owner generated these environment illustrations with an external
image tool and supplied the original JPEGs for this Trinket expansion on
November 25, 2026. The project owner directed their use in Trinket. The
generator and its terms were not supplied.

- Crimson Vale: `Crimson Vale.jpg`, adopted as `Crimson Vale - Portrait.jpeg`
- Sun Abbey: `Sun Abbey.jpg`, adopted as `Sun Abbey - Portrait.jpeg`
- Shattered Pass: `Shattered Pass.jpg`, adopted as `Shattered Pass - Portrait.jpeg`

The selected files are under `AssetSources/2d Assets/Game Sources/Places/Regions/`.
All three are 1792 × 2400 originals copied without pixel edits. The curated art
manifest owns their shipping crops.
