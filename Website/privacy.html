Privacy policy

Last updated November 1, 2026

Trinket, developed by Ryan McIntire, does not require a Trinket account and does not use advertising or cross-app tracking.

Your saved game

Your progress, characters, equipment, and preferences are stored on your device. When iCloud progress sync is enabled in your build and you are signed in to iCloud, saved progress also syncs through your private iCloud storage across devices using the same Apple Account. You can keep playing offline; changes sync after you reconnect. No Trinket account is required, and the developer does not operate a game-save server.

You can reset gameplay progress in Options. When linked to iCloud, the reset propagates to synced devices, but progress from an offline device can return when it reconnects. Resetting does not remove Full Game ownership. Deleting the app removes local data but may leave saved progress in iCloud; you can manage the app's iCloud data in your device's settings.

Purchases

Apple processes In-App Purchases using your Apple Account. Trinket receives verified purchase information through StoreKit to determine whether Full Game is available, including eligible Family Sharing access. Trinket does not receive your payment-card details or Apple Account password.

Diagnostics

Trinket uses Apple’s system diagnostics to help identify crashes and performance issues. It does not send gameplay analytics to a developer-operated server. Apple may make diagnostics available according to your device’s privacy and analytics settings.

Support messages

If you contact support, the developer receives the email address and information you choose to send and uses them to respond and investigate the issue. These messages are not sold or used for advertising. Contact support to request deletion; information may be retained where necessary to resolve an ongoing issue or meet a legal obligation.

This website

These pages use no analytics scripts, advertising, forms, or cookies added by Trinket. The website is hosted on GitHub Pages; GitHub may process technical request information under its privacy statement.

Questions

Contact rymcintire@gmail.com about privacy or support.

This policy will be updated if Trinket’s data practices change. Apple’s privacy policy describes Apple’s handling of iCloud, App Store, and diagnostic information.
