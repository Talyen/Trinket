twin-casting.png · iPhone · 1180 × 2376 — The unique staff Twin Casting draws a Freeze card when you spend Mana to empower a Burn card, and vice versa.
saintfall-plate.png · iPhone Simulator · 1206 × 2622 — The first time each round the wearer’s Block breaks, Saintfall Plate deals Holy and Stun damage to the attacker and restores Health.
