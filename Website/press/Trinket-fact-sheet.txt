Trinket! | Press fact sheet

Trinket! is a turn-based card RPG where your hero and companion share a hand of cards.

Pair a hero with a companion and share one hand of cards each turn, combining their abilities, gear, and talent trees to set up combos across four Campaign chapters, from the Forest to the Tundra. Each of the seven bosses features an original vocal theme with live lyrics in combat.

Beyond the Campaign, climb seven Spires, push into an endless Labyrinth, chart a Voyage, or take a single-fight Contract with its reward shown up front. Between runs, build up your Homestead to grow herbs, forge gear, and strengthen your roster.

Game content at launch

Modes and world
Campaign: Four chapters with 10 stages each. Play Forest, Dungeon and Desert for free. The full game adds Tundra.
Spires: Seven Spires with 20 floors each (140 floors total). The first 10 floors of every Spire are free.
Labyrinth: An endless dungeon. The first three floors are free.
Contracts: Three single-battle jobs on the board at a time. Contracts are unlimited in free play.
Voyage: Repeatable routes of 8, 10 or 12 stops. Forest, Dungeon and Desert are free. The full game adds Tundra.
Enemies: 39 enemies, including 7 bosses.
Homestead: 20 projects across Farming, Crafting, Alchemy, Training and Arcana. Together they have 80 tiers, including 20 initial builds and 60 upgrades.

Roster and progression
Heroes: 8 heroes. Ranger, Knight, Rogue, Wizard, Druid, Warlock, Alchemist, Wildcard.
Companions: 13 companions. Wolf, Bear, Frost Whelp, Lizard Scout, Panther, Phoenix, Golden Retriever, Library Owl, Risen Skeleton, Mana Moth, Pixie, Shield Scarab, Fox.
Abilities: 65 distinct equippable abilities across the roster, including 20 basics, 26 skills and 19 ultimates.
Talents: 446 talents across 63 trees, three trees per character. The Wizard can specialize in Cryomancy, Pyromancy or Arcana.
Equipment: Basic and Astral gear draws from 111 item affixes. Basic gear rolls 1 to 2 affixes per item and Astral gear rolls 3 to 4. There are also 24 trinkets and 29 unique items.
Crafting: 26 equipment recipes. The Homestead offers 14 Blacksmith Forge recipes, 8 Archery Range recipes and 4 Wool Tailoring recipes.

Free play includes the Knight, Ranger, Rogue and Wizard, plus the Wolf, Bear, Frost Whelp and Library Owl. Each has their full ability and talent progression. A full-game purchase opens the rest of the roster for recruitment.

Where to find the Blood Countess: Explore → Spires → Sanguine Court, floor 10. This boss is within the free portion of the Spire. The fight starts at 0:58 in the feature trailer.
