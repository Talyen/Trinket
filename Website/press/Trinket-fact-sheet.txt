Trinket! | Press fact sheet

Trinket! is a turn-based card RPG where your hero and companion share a hand of cards. Freeze an enemy with your Wizard, then play your Frost Whelp’s Ice Shot for double damage. You choose the abilities they bring to battle and the order you play them.

Between fights, check the Contracts board. Each of the three offers shows the enemy and a bonus reward. Pick a job for materials you need at the Homestead, or see how your pair fares against a boss.

Recruit new companions in the Campaign, tend the Herb Garden at the Homestead, or send your party on a Desert Voyage. You can also climb the Spires and explore the Labyrinth.

Developer: Ryan McIntire, solo indie developer
Release: December 21, 2026 · United States
Platform: iPhone and iPad · iOS / iPadOS 18 or later
Genre: Single-player fantasy RPG with turn-based card combat
Price: Free download. Play the first three Campaign chapters free. One US$4.99 purchase unlocks the full game.
Free play: Also includes unlimited Contracts, three Labyrinth floors, ten floors of every Spire and Voyages in the Forest, Dungeon and Desert.
Offline: Play offline. No Trinket account required. Optional iCloud save sync.

Where to find the Blood Countess: Explore → Spires → Sanguine Court, floor 10. This boss is within the free portion of the Spire. The fight starts at 0:58 in the feature trailer.
