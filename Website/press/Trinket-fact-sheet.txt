Trinket! | Press fact sheet

Pair a hero with a companion and build the hand they take into battle in this solo-developed fantasy card RPG for iPhone and iPad.

A Wizard and a Frost Whelp head into Sanguine Court. The Wizard brings Ray of Frost. The Blood Countess brings a song of her own. In Trinket!, the cards you take into a turn-based fight come from the hero and companion you pair up and the abilities you choose for them.

Recruit a Bear and its Sunder can cut an enemy’s Block in half before it hits. Between fights, tend the Herb Garden, compare the rewards on the Contracts board, or send a party on a Desert Voyage. The same roster can tackle the Campaign, climb boss Spires and explore the Labyrinth.

Developer: Ryan McIntire, solo indie developer
Release: December 21, 2026 · United States
Platform: iPhone and iPad · iOS / iPadOS 18 or later
Genre: Single-player fantasy RPG with turn-based card combat
Price: First three Campaign chapters free. One permanent Full Game purchase, US$4.99.
Free play: Also includes unlimited Contracts, three Labyrinth floors, ten floors of every Spire and Voyages in the Forest, Dungeon and Desert.
Offline: Play offline without an account. Optional iCloud save sync.
