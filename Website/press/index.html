About the game

Choose a hero and a companion, play cards from both in one hand, then use the gear you find and the upgrades you build at the Homestead to prepare for the next fight.

A Knight paired with a Bear can bring Sunder to cut an enemy’s Block in half before the Bear strikes. Pair her with a Frost Whelp instead and Ray of Frost can hit twice with a single card.

Between fights, tend the Herb Garden, compare the rewards on the Contracts board, or send a party on a Desert Voyage. The same roster can tackle the Campaign, climb boss Spires and explore the Labyrinth.
