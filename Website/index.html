What does Full Game include?

Full Game is a permanent, one-time purchase covering all chapters and game modes, every hero and companion, and future content released within Trinket. Characters and upgrades are still earned through play. Future content is included if released; there is no promised update schedule.

Free play includes the first three campaign chapters, the first three Labyrinth floors, the first ten floors of every Spire, unlimited Contracts, and a starter roster. Equipment, Homestead, and the full ability and talent progression of accessible characters remain available.

Restore a purchase

Open Trinket → Options → Restore Purchases. Use the Apple Account that originally purchased Full Game. An internet connection may be needed to restore access on a new installation.

Trinket uses Apple’s In-App Purchase system. Apple handles payment confirmation and billing; Trinket never asks for your card details.

Family Sharing

Full Game supports Apple Family Sharing for eligible family members. Check your purchase-sharing settings, then use Restore Purchases in Trinket. Sharing access does not share your saved game.

Saved progress

Progress is saved on your device. Where iCloud synchronization is enabled, it uses your private iCloud storage. Gameplay reset clears progress but does not remove Full Game ownership. Deleting the app can remove local progress, so do not reinstall as a first troubleshooting step.

Refunds and missing access

Apple handles refund requests at Report a Problem. A refund or a change in Family Sharing can remove premium access. Trinket retains earned progress so it is available if access is restored.

If restoration doesn’t help, contact support before resetting or reinstalling.
