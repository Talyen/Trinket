What makes Trinket! distinct

Pair a hero with a companion

Every turn, your hero and companion draw from one shared hand of cards. Mix Burn and Freeze combos, stack Bleed and Poison, or anchor the front line with Briar Shield and Smite.

Build your party from 8 heroes and 13 companions, with 65 equippable abilities and 446 talents across 63 trees.

Five modes, seven singing bosses

Journey through four Campaign chapters from the Forest to the Tundra, where each of the seven bosses performs an original vocal theme with live lyrics in combat.

Beyond the Campaign, climb 7 Spires totaling 140 floors, delve into an endless Labyrinth, chart repeatable Voyages, or take a single-battle Contract with its reward shown up front.

Grow your Homestead between runs

Return home to tend herbs, forge weapons, and invest in 20 Homestead projects across Farming, Crafting, Alchemy, Training, and Arcana.

Hunt for Astral gear drawn from 111 item affixes, 24 trinkets, and 29 build-defining unique items, or craft new gear from 26 Homestead recipes.
